# Grand Mathematical Story Archetypes

This profile is a discovery aid for research Introductions. It is not a paragraph template and not a phrase bank.

## Core principle

A high-level mathematical story is a **structural relation among theorems and regimes**. It is not a list of prestigious subjects.

The strongest story usually identifies:

`canonical principle → restricted/critical/finite setting → exact obstruction → new theorem → changed program-level picture`.

## High-value story motions

### Continuum to finite complexity
A classical continuum optimizer or symmetry principle predicts a discrete/finite-complexity candidate, but the operation that proves the continuum result does not preserve the restricted class.

### Limiting to exact finite
A sequence of admissible objects converges to a rigid limit, but convergence alone does not decide which finite object is extremal. The missing mathematics is a gap estimate, stability theorem, or exact rigidity mechanism.

### Local to global
Perturbative theory around the candidate is understood, but global moduli contain degeneration, topology changes, or nonlocal competitors. The paper supplies global compactness, exclusion, or continuation.

### Qualitative to quantitative
An inequality or uniqueness theorem is known, but stability requires identifying the correct deficit, topology, and sharp scale.

### Symmetry to rigidity
Symmetry suggests the candidate and may even generate stationary solutions, yet equality classification needs a mechanism that propagates or forces symmetry.

### Critical transition
The familiar theory works on one side of a parameter/dimension/exponent threshold and fails at the endpoint. The paper resolves the mechanism replacing the lost compactness/coercivity/monotonicity.

### Family and missing member
A natural family of functionals or geometric problems exhibits a common extremizer; one member remains unresolved because its variational structure is different. The paper closes or clarifies that missing member.

## Large-N polygonal Pólya–Szegő benchmark

A strong story does not begin from the torsion functional alone. It can place the problem inside the classical isoperimetric program: unrestricted shape optimization selects the disk; imposing exactly N sides destroys the continuous admissible class and replaces rotational symmetry by finite dihedral symmetry; as N grows the regular polygon approaches the disk, but limiting convergence does not imply exact finite-N global optimality; the real theorem must recover finite-complexity rigidity from a theory whose natural benchmark is continuous. This benchmark is an example of the `CONTINUUM_TO_FINITE_COMPLEXITY` plus `LIMIT_TO_EXACT_FINITE` archetypes.

Do not reuse this exact story for unrelated polygon papers. Extract only the structural logic when supported.
