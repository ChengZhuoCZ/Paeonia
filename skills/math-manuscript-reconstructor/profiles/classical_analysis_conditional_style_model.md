# Classical Analysis Conditional Style Model — High-Intensity Research Projection

For Introduction/outline work, [Protocol 04p](../protocols/04p_reformulation_and_proof_explanation.md) qualifies research compression: retain necessary explanations of novel objects, reformulation gains, and proof dependencies.

## Status

This model is distilled from the full user-supplied Markdown analysis corpus. It is a **conditional microstyle model**, not an author imitation profile. The source corpus controls how prose reacts to mathematical function; it does not supply sentences, paragraph skeletons, or research-priority rhetoric.

## Evidence base

A full-corpus pass produced 7,185 usable prose paragraphs across role-conditioned strata. Eight contiguous holdout folds were used to distinguish stable tendencies from chapter-local accidents. Numerical values are evidence only and are never generation targets.

The strongest stable evidence is not a global average. It is the separation between mathematical roles.

## 1. Proof pressure covariance

### Routine proof motion

Routine proof paragraphs are compact and direct. They favor concrete mathematical movement over commentary. Standard manipulations receive little prose unless a local convention or hypothesis matters.

### Delicate passages

Limiting arguments, regularity changes, approximation passages, endpoint issues, equality/uniqueness steps, and similar delicate moves are consistently thicker than routine proof motion. Their sentences carry more conditional structure because the prose records legality and hypotheses.

### Technical estimates

Long estimates receive local explanatory mass, but the prose remains causal: target quantity, regime split, source of bounds, assembly. The style does not narrate every algebraic line.

### Bridges

Changes of method receive their own explanatory pressure. The preceding mechanism's exact limitation is stated before the replacement mechanism enters.

### Closure

Closures shorten again. Once the mathematical target is secured, exposition stops.

## 2. Theorem ecology

Theorem lead-ins are comparatively compact. They orient the reader toward the mathematical threshold for the statement rather than producing ceremonial announcement prose.

Auxiliary results are introduced when a proof requires a mechanism, identity, approximation fact, or reusable obstruction-removal device. The text returns to the main line after the auxiliary role is discharged.

## 3. Section-opening behavior

Section openings receive more room than routine proof paragraphs, but their additional mass is mathematical rather than administrative. The object, question, mechanism, or conceptual shift normally carries the opening. Authorial narration is not the primary source of coherence.

## 4. Conceptual interpretation

Interpretive paragraphs are neither one-line slogans nor full recaps. They isolate one geometric/analytic meaning that reduces the cognitive cost of what follows.

## 5. Sentence architecture

Across stable proof strata:

- medium sentences dominate routine movement;
- delicate, technical, and bridge contexts lengthen modestly and increase clause load;
- first-person plural remains available for genuine actions but does not become the universal sentence subject;
- administrative openers are sparse relative to mathematical entry modes;
- causal and conditional clauses carry hypotheses, legality, and consequences rather than decorative rhetoric.

## 6. High-intensity Introduction microstyle prior

The Markdown corpus contains only limited Introduction-like material and is textbook/monograph prose. Therefore it supplies **microstyle evidence with restricted authority**.

Retain for research Introductions:

- object-led and theorem-led paragraph entry;
- longer sentences only when they connect real mathematical scales;
- low administrative-opener dependence;
- claim-centered transitions;
- explicit obstruction/replacement movement;
- conceptual interpretation that prepares the next paragraph.

Suppress in the research projection:

- elementary definitions for specialists;
- reader coaching;
- broad pedagogical history not tied to the theorem;
- exercise/lecture sequencing;
- textbook recaps.

Macro narrative, literature positioning, theorem scale, and editorial ambition must come from the research-Introduction engine and verified literature, not from this corpus.

## 7. Realization invariant

**Match the covariance, not the numbers.**

The intended effect is that routine mathematics feels compressed, delicate mathematics receives visible legality, method changes receive explicit bridges, section openings breathe more, and theorem/Introduction prose uses the appropriate local pressure. No paragraph is rewritten to match a source word count or sentence count.

## 8. Source independence

This model stores no source sentence, phrase bank, source paragraph, source role sequence, or author persona. Final prose remains subject to style-source overlap auditing.
