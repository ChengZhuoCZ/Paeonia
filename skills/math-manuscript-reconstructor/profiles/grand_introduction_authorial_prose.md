# Grand Introduction Authorial Prose Profile

## Scope

This profile governs the prose realization of a research-mathematics Introduction after the theorem and narrative architecture are already fixed. It is distilled from abstract traits of the designated research reference and the existing classical-analysis prose profile. It stores no source sentences.

## Core register

- research-specialist rather than textbook-expository;
- broad in mathematical range but economical in definitions;
- result-centered rather than author-centered;
- historically aware without becoming a chronological survey;
- explicit about obstruction and method necessity;
- calm in rhetoric even when the mathematical scale is large;
- willing to use strong, exact result language when verified rather than euphemizing the contribution.

## Paragraph rhythm

Different paragraph roles have visibly different pressure.

- field orientation: compact and directional;
- classical/frontier positioning: moderately dense, claim-centered;
- obstruction: locally thicker because the mechanism failure must be explained;
- theorem bridge: short;
- theorem interpretation: selective, never a formula paraphrase;
- proof mechanism: compact but causally connected.

Do not make every paragraph follow topic-sentence → citations → slogan.

## Opening voice

Open through a mathematical object, problem, landmark theorem, conjecture, structural contrast, or phenomenon. Avoid ceremonial phrases and elementary setup unless the target audience genuinely needs them.

## Literature voice

Mathematical claims are the grammatical center. Authors and citations support those claims. Several works may be grouped when they play the same mathematical role. Chronology is used only when it explains a change in the frontier.

## Transition voice

Transitions name relations: implication, obstruction, contrast, strengthening, return, or method need. Administrative transitions are compressed or removed.

## Theorem voice

Orientation before the theorem is concise. Formal theorem statements carry precision. Prose after the theorem explains consequence, scope, or comparison—not the displayed formula again.

## Calibrated ambition

Use scale, history, conjectural position, exact prior frontier, theorem strength, and mechanism to create breadth. If a strong contribution is verified, state it directly. Avoid prestige adjectives as substitutes for mathematics, but do not translate `resolves`, `complete`, `sharp`, or `first global` into weaker generic language merely for tone.

## Anti-copy rule

Preserve only the abstract narrative and prose decisions. Do not reproduce the reference paper's paragraph sequence, citation sequence, theorem order, distinctive wording, or sentence skeletons.
