# v1.26 instruction review

This is a same-agent review of editorial choices. It is not a blind generation trial, an independent reader test, or a mathematical audit. The evidence is the user's explicit selection of the source Introduction, the earlier interface comparison, and inspection of the changed instructions.

## Decisions exercised

| Situation | Chosen behavior and reason | Rejected transfer |
|---|---|---|
| The preferred source explains the inverse-cubic energy through the reconstruction and the Hessian | Preserve a visible derivation and interpretation; it answers why the measure enters the spectral argument | Merely increase adjective frequency or state that the reformulation is nontrivial |
| A previous outline contains many correct stage descriptions but its main relation is hard to retain | Give the relation a continuous conceptual explanation, then group the remaining proof work by its output | Treat more individually explained lemmas as an automatic improvement |
| The same cubic functional appears before and after mesh control | Distinguish recovery of the common leading value from a later defect-relative estimate because their prerequisites differ | Compress both into a single cancellation step, or require two cubic steps in other papers |
| A new packet has an order-q Hessian and reconstruction multiplier of order -r | Let the supplied operators determine a quadratic weight of order q-2r; expose this calculation if it explains the formulation | Copy exponent 3, positivity, a lattice equality case, or a nonlinear estimate from the polygonal sample without supporting results |
| A short supplied proof closes directly by a sharp inequality and its equality case | Explain the decisive inequality and recovery of equality directly | Manufacture a surprising bridge, a bootstrap, or an extra introductory theorem to match the sample |
| The user freezes the first three pages and permits edits only in the fourth | Preserve the protected prose and make the authorized local clarification, documenting any unresolved limits | Generalize the resulting late convention paragraph into the default placement of assumptions |
| A preferred paragraph states a complex estimate but the body establishes only its real part | Preserve the useful narrative and repair the scope of the displayed claim when authorized | Treat user preference as proof certification, or erase the entire narrative because one interface needed correction |

## Review result

The changed routes select both expansion and compression for a specific reader inference. They preserve the existing exact-versus-principal comparison, source-fidelity, bootstrap-entry, and small-defect requirements. The new profile is explicitly one user's positive example, not corpus-wide evidence. The instructions do not prescribe phrases, page counts, a formula count, a fixed section sequence, or mathematical mechanisms.

Remaining empirical question: whether a fresh writer using these instructions produces an Introduction the user prefers on an unseen manuscript. This release does not claim that experiment has been performed. Existing deterministic tests cover package compatibility and evidence/dependency structures only.
