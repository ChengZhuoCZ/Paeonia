# Generative Residue Checklist

Review, do not mechanically rewrite:

- Are unrelated proofs opened with the same sentence frame?
- Are closure phrases repeated where the last formula already closes the proof?
- Does any display receive a prose translation instead of a consequence?
- Are `We now/next/proceed/turn` phrases carrying no mathematical reason?
- Are `important/crucial/key/fundamental` labels used without identifying the role?
- Do several proofs have identical Step geometry without identical dependency geometry?
- Are routine and delicate passages written at nearly the same density?
- Are first-person sentences describing document administration rather than mathematical action?
- Are repeated 3–5 word prose phrases concentrated enough to create a visible tic?
- Has any rewrite used random synonym replacement, random sentence splitting, fake uncertainty, deliberate typos, or artificial colloquialism?

Pass only after each flagged recurrence has been classified `KEEP`, `DELETE`, `COMPRESS`, `MATHEMATICALIZE`, or `RESTRUCTURE`.
