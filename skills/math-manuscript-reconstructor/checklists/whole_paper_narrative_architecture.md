# Whole-Paper Narrative Architecture Checklist

- Is the principal result stated as early as it can be read accurately?
- Is proof activation distinguished from theorem statement exposure?
- Is there a single identifiable proof spine or an explicitly justified multi-spine topology?
- Do technical peaks correspond to actual difficult engines/regimes?
- Has any section been padded or compressed for visual balance?
- Does the Introduction compress the proof mechanism instead of replaying local estimates?
- Is the canonical main theorem statement duplicated unnecessarily?
- Does every appendix have an owner, imported result, and modularity reason?
- Is central novelty/equality/rigidity kept in the main reader-facing spine?
- Are source-wide counts, ratios, lengths, and sequences treated only as evidence?
- Is a conclusion section present only if it adds mathematical value?
- Does the paper stop after the final meaningful mathematical event?
