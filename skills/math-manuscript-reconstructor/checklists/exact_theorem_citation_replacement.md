# Exact-theorem citation replacement — proof editing checklist

Apply to **substantive candidates within authorized proof-editing scope**, not to every displayed equation.

- [ ] Existing proof-body candidate was identified from the actual proof graph; original claim and downstream uses were written down at full strength.
- [ ] A primary source full text, not an abstract/search result or secondary bibliography, was opened.
- [ ] Source author/title/URL or DOI and exact publication edition or arXiv version match the inspected theorem.
- [ ] The real *kind* (`Theorem`, `Lemma`, `Proposition`, `Corollary` etc.) and actual number were verified; an unnumbered statement has an unambiguous name/page.
- [ ] The theorem statement itself was read; metadata, locator and claim support were checked separately.
- [ ] Every nonautomatic external hypothesis is established at a precise earlier local fact or verified imported fact.
- [ ] Object, operator, boundary conditions, dimension, class, regularity and normalization agree or have explicit translations.
- [ ] Parameter quantifiers, constant dependencies, uniformity, asymptotic rates, endpoints and strictness are not strengthened.
- [ ] Equality, rigidity, uniqueness and sharpness are separately covered or remain locally proved.
- [ ] The external theorem is logically independent of the target claim; no citation cycle was introduced.
- [ ] Direct/adapted/partial status matches the *actual* theorem coverage; unmatched proof steps remain.
- [ ] Short indispensable conceptual explanation remains; no citation-padding quota or unjustified one-line-proof replacement.
- [ ] New text uses an exact checked locator (`\cite[Theorem~…]{…}` or equivalent) plus a concise application, not `\cite{paper}` alone.
- [ ] Downstream labels, equations, Intro/Abstract method credit, bibliography and cross-references remain coherent.
- [ ] Protected regions were not rewritten; an unverified candidate was left as an original proof, not applied.
- [ ] If used, the dossier checker passed *structural* validation; no mathematical certification was inferred from it.
