# Authorial Mathematical Prose Checklist

For each proof or major proof block:

- Is the opening the actual reduction/construction/obstruction rather than an administrative preview?
- Are routine steps compressed?
- Are delicate legality and uniformity issues explicit at point of use?
- Does a method change state the obstruction that causes it?
- Does every isolated lemma have a dependency reason?
- Do transitions encode cause, reduction, regime change, limit passage, rigidity, or assembly?
- Are displays followed by consequences rather than paraphrases?
- Is first-person plural reserved mainly for genuine mathematical actions?
- Does proof length and paragraph density reflect mathematical load?
- Does the proof stop at its mathematical endpoint?
- Has the cold-author pass removed sentences that merely announce mathematics?
- Does the final prose still satisfy the distilled style ledger after compression?
