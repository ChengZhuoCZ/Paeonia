# Section Narrative Architecture Checklist

For each section:

- [ ] The boundary is justified by a section-scale mathematical state change.
- [ ] The section has a coherent obligation family or reader-facing purpose.
- [ ] Its theorem/lemma cluster has downstream life or a genuine local engine; discovery history alone is not the reason for grouping.
- [ ] The principal theorem is stated as early as its prerequisites permit, but not earlier.
- [ ] The opening identifies a goal, problem, object, obstruction, application, or precise pivot.
- [ ] Technical density is interrupted by conceptual orientation only when the mathematical state changes.
- [ ] No conceptual/technical alternation was imposed mechanically.
- [ ] Every subsection names a stable internal phase.
- [ ] No subsection exists merely to break length or disguise a proof step.
- [ ] A one-subsection section has been reviewed for unnecessary hierarchy.
- [ ] The section closes when its obligation family closes.
- [ ] No generic summary paragraph was added without a mathematical function.
- [ ] Section count, length, theorem latency, and subsection rate were not tuned to the style corpus.
- [ ] The source section order and role sequence were not reused as a template.
