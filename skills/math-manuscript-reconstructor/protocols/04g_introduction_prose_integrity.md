# Protocol 04g — Introduction Prose Integrity Audit

## Purpose

**From-scratch realization compatibility.** In `RECONSTRUCT`, prose is newly composed from the mathematics and verified literature; this does not reinstate the old mandatory paragraph-plan stack. `LOCAL_POLISH` instead preserves the selected baseline.

`INTRODUCTION-PROSE-INTEGRITY` checks whether the final Introduction is mathematically specific, reader-complete, and authorial. It does not reward the number of ledgers, paragraph roles, historical layers, or named mechanisms used.

Protocol 04 and the `IntroductionCoreBrief` are authoritative. Grand-narrative, mathematical-story, scientific-context, and editorial-impact conditions apply only when their modules were genuinely triggered.

## Pass conditions

Pass only if the applicable conditions hold:

1. **Semantic fidelity.** The problem, theorem, hypotheses, parameter range, equality case, and scope match the body.
2. **Reader contract.** The seven questions in Protocol 04f are recoverable from the Introduction alone.
3. **Specific opening.** The opening establishes a concrete mathematical stake rather than transferable field-level ceremony.
4. **Theorem latency.** The main result appears as soon as it is accurately readable; every earlier paragraph is indispensable.
5. **Information-changing paragraphs.** Paragraphs change the reader's mathematical state. They are not ledger roles converted into prose.
6. **Research-backed, fairly attributed literature.** For reconstruction, the literature was searched beyond the inherited `.bib` via 04u where external access was possible; the Introduction clearly attributes origin, precise predecessors, partial/parallel work and borrowed methods rather than letting the present authors appear to originate them. Citations establish benchmark, frontier, obstruction, precursor, or a useful neighboring result.
7. **Concrete obstruction.** When nonroutine difficulty matters, it is stated through the failed property or lost structure, not a prestige adjective.
8. **Distinct theorem functions.** Orientation, formal statement, and interpretation do not duplicate one another.
9. **Conceptual proof resolution.** The overview exposes the central hinge and stage interfaces while compressing bookkeeping.
10. **Calibrated strength.** Verified strong conclusions are stated directly at exact scope; unsupported inflation is absent.
11. **Authorial continuity.** Transitions follow mathematical questions and referents rather than administrative phrases.
12. **Economy.** No detachable background, literature, adjacent-problem, or ceremonial ending paragraph remains.
13. **Reference independence or scoped preservation.** Reconstruction does not copy a reference's choreography; local polishing preserves the selected authorial baseline.
14. **Program integrity.** The 04v program test is recorded for reconstruction; any included grand or scientific context changes theorem legibility or interpretation and returns to the focal problem. The larger-story modules remain conditional.

## Automatic blockers

Flag `FAIL_INTRODUCTION_PROSE` for any of the following:

- the exact problem, pre-paper gap, or main theorem cannot be recovered from the Introduction;
- a substantial paragraph survives the swap test—that is, it could fit many unrelated papers after only the subject nouns are replaced;
- the theorem is delayed by detachable history, applications, chronology, or prestige framing;
- literature is mainly a list of authors, dates, or repeated statements that earlier work does not cover the present case;
- a reconstructed Introduction claims a current or comprehensive frontier despite searching only inherited bibliography items or having no live access;
- a close verifiable predecessor or independent parallel contribution is omitted in a way that falsely assigns its work to the present paper;
- grand language is used without the verified parent-program and structural-tension bridge;
- post-theorem prose merely paraphrases the display;
- the proof overview is a section itinerary, technique catalogue, or list of proof-local labels;
- a central representation is named without its original meaning, mathematical gain, or remaining cost;
- multiple ledgers or optional modules have produced repeated `larger story → narrowing → re-expansion` prose without new mathematical information;
- a grand/program-level paragraph is present only because the journal target is ambitious;
- scientific context functions as wallpaper;
- the Introduction ends by repeating the Abstract;
- local polishing replaces effective authorial prose without a demonstrated reader or correctness gain.

## Repair routing

Repair the earliest failed reader inference:

- problem/frontier/theorem mismatch → `IntroductionCoreBrief`;
- weak opening or delayed theorem → narrative-arc selection;
- generic paragraphs → swap-test rewrite with a specific relation;
- missing, miscredited or unverified scholarship → Protocol 04u discovery/credit map and Protocol 07 primary-source checks;
- literature survey drift → claim-centered literature map;
- opaque proof route → Protocol 04p, limited to the conceptual bottleneck;
- under- or over-positioning → Protocol 04h/04j only when evidence supports that trigger;
- sentence rhythm or generative residue → Protocols 05c–05f after mathematical structure is stable.

Do not repair weak prose by adding another mandatory ledger or broadening the historical frame by default.
