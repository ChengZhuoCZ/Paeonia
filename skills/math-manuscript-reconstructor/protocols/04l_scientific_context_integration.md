# Protocol 04l — Scientific Context Integration into the Introduction

## Purpose

Integrate `ScientificContextLedger` into the research Introduction without producing either textbook exposition or decorative physics wallpaper.

## 1. Two-axis architecture

Combine:

- `NarrativeAmbitionLedger.scale`: STANDARD / EXPANSIVE / GRAND / GRAND_PLUS;
- `ScientificContextLedger.context_depth`: NONE / ORIGIN_BRIEF / SUBSTANTIAL / PHYSICS_LED / INTERDISCIPLINARY_LED.

Do not collapse these into one score.

## 2. Opening modes

When supported, the Introduction may open from:

- `PHYSICAL_PHENOMENON` — the phenomenon whose idealization produces the focal mathematics;
- `MODEL_ORIGIN` — a canonical model or variational principle;
- `MATHEMATICAL_PHYSICS_LANDMARK` — a classical theorem born from the scientific problem;
- `DISCRETE_CONTINUUM_TENSION` — a continuous model placed under finite-complexity/discrete constraints;
- `SCIENTIFIC_REGIME` — a distinguished scaling, critical mass, high-frequency, dilute, semiclassical, or other physically meaningful regime.

These modes are alternatives to formula-first or definition-first openings. Use them only when the model-to-theorem bridge is real.

## 3. Paragraph roles

The paragraph plan may use:

- `SCIENTIFIC_PHENOMENON`;
- `MODEL_ORIGIN`;
- `MODEL_TO_MATHEMATICS`;
- `PHYSICAL_PARAMETER_REGIME`;
- `MATHEMATICAL_PHYSICS_LANDMARK`;
- `SCIENTIFIC_TO_MATHEMATICAL_FRONTIER`;
- `PHYSICAL_INTERPRETATION_OF_THEOREM`;
- `SCIENTIFIC_RE_EXPANSION`.

Each paragraph must still have one dominant role and an explicit return anchor to the mathematical problem.

## 4. Substantial physical background

For `SUBSTANTIAL` or stronger context, a multi-paragraph scientific opening is allowed and sometimes preferred. The sequence may be:

`phenomenon → model/energy → parameter/scaling → mathematical structure → classical theorem/frontier → focal problem`.

Do not compress this merely because the audience is mathematically sophisticated. Compress only what is scientifically standard **and** irrelevant to the theorem.

## 5. Physics wallpaper test

A scientific paragraph fails if all scientifically loaded sentences can be deleted without changing:

- the meaning of the focal equation;
- the naturality of its regime/constraint;
- the historical position of the problem;
- the interpretation of the theorem;
- or the proof mechanism.

Such a paragraph is decorative and must be removed or rebuilt.

## 6. Theorem translation

After the formal theorem, allow a calibrated scientific interpretation when the model map supports one. Translate only the part of the theorem that has a genuine model meaning.

Do not claim experimental validation, engineering optimality, or physical universality from a purely mathematical theorem unless separately justified.

## 7. Re-expansion

For GRAND/GRAND_PLUS papers, re-expand after the theorem along the strongest justified axis:

- mathematical program;
- mathematical-physics lineage;
- model class;
- scientific phenomenon;
- transferable mechanism.

The re-expansion should make the result feel larger by revealing its actual context, not by adding prestige adjectives.

## 8. Large-N / discrete-continuum pattern

When a theorem concerns a finite-complexity approximation of a continuous extremal problem, explicitly test whether the Introduction should expose the axis

`continuous optimizer → discrete admissible class → asymptotic localization → exact discrete rigidity`.

If the underlying spectral quantity has a classical physical interpretation, that interpretation may form the historical entry point. This pattern is a decision rule, not a prose template.


## Mathematical-story coexistence

Scientific context and the larger mathematical story are independent axes. When `MathematicalStoryLedger` is required, choose explicitly whether the Introduction is `MATH_LED_WITH_SCIENCE_ORIGIN`, `INTERLEAVED`, `MATH_ONLY_MAINLINE`, or `SCIENCE_LED_WITH_MATH_PROGRAM`. Even a physics-led Introduction must identify the parent mathematical program before the contribution is interpreted.
