## Core rule

Formulaic prose is repaired by exposing mathematical function, not by lexical randomization.

Flag repeated templates such as:
- `We now proceed to prove ...`;
- `The proof consists of the following steps.`;
- `It is worth noting that ...`;
- repeated `Clearly/Obviously` without a visible reason;
- `Combining the above ...` without naming what the combination achieves;
- mechanical `This completes the proof.` endings.

None is forbidden in isolation. Repetition and lack of mathematical function are the problem.

## Preferred action

Replace a meta-transition by the actual relation when needed, otherwise delete it.

Example of the principle:
- administrative: announces that an estimate comes next;
- mathematical: identifies which uncontrolled term the estimate handles.

## Non-randomness rule

Do not vary proof openings, transitions, or endings by rotating synonyms. Variation must arise from changes in proof role, mathematical subject, regime, or logical relation.
