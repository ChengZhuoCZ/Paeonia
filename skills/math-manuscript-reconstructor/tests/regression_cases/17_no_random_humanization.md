# Regression 17 — No random humanization

## Forbidden strategies

- random synonym substitution;
- random sentence shortening;
- random paragraph splitting;
- deliberate grammar errors or typos;
- fake uncertainty;
- fake personal voice;
- artificial colloquialism;
- random connector rotation;
- optimization against an AI-detector score.

## Required behavior

Natural variation may arise only from mathematical role, dependency, logical relation, or information load.
