# Editorial decision review — v1.23.0

Method: same-agent qualitative review of concrete alternatives, not a fresh-agent run or human-reader experiment. The comparison is between decisions permitted by the revised protocols, not measured outcomes on a population of readers. No automatic test is claimed to establish prose quality.

| Situation considered | Decision made under revised rule | Evidence for the decision |
|---|---|---|
| The outline names turning, rigidity, and cancellation but says nothing about k^-3 | Reject the apparent completeness; expose the turning-to-Hessian bridge | Without it the reader cannot predict the selected energy; Case A supplies the missing calculation |
| More stage headings are proposed for that same passage | Reject as the first repair | The missing inference is unchanged by the headings |
| The outline says only that cyclic terms yield o(E) | Open this bottleneck using the paired phase/weight errors | Case B and the target eq:relative-cubic-008–011 identify the operation that preserves a full defect factor |
| The same proof invokes a routine known compactness statement with its hypotheses visible | Retain a compact black box | No missing conceptual inference justifies expanding every module |
| A paragraph announces a surprising spectral–sampling connection and explains nodes, weights, and the spectral exponent | Permit and, under the user's expressive request, retain the evaluative opening | Case C identifies both the mathematical referent and its explanation; no priority claim is implied |
| A paragraph calls a method remarkable and broadly connected but identifies no objects | Reject the paragraph as unexplained; supply the connection, not a synonym | “Central” in place of “remarkable” would leave the same failure |
| An interpretation says E_N is the norm of ν−σ_N | Reject that interpretation | The dossier identifies E_N as half the difference of squared quadrature errors, with explicit Fourier normalization |
| A novelty claim rests on allowing unequal charges | Reject this proposed historical distinction | Cohn–Kumar §4 already discusses unequal charges; the spectral bridge and quantitative target obligations require separate positioning |
| A link to universal optimality is presented as proving the full eigenvalue theorem | Reject the automatic transfer | The exact Bessel correction and nonlinear remainder are outside the cited general energy result |
| A proposed outline model assumes the mesh regularity the argument must first obtain | Restrict the model to illustrating the later estimate; preserve the independent entry argument | Otherwise it deletes the central obstruction rather than explaining it |
| A self-review explains the cutoff from memory but the prose only says “use exact coordinates” | Record a failed text-level inference and repair the passage | Case F distinguishes the correctness of the remembered explanation from its presence in the outline |
| A short direct argument is assigned a six-stage outline | Reject the fixed scaffold | The same reader-inference principle calls for fewer interventions when the argument is already transparent |

The review supports the specificity and conditional nature of the new guidance. Actual future manuscript prose must still be read under Protocol 04q. The present task updates the skill; it does not replace the delivered v1.22 manuscript or claim that new prose has already passed an independent reader test.
