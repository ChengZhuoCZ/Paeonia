# Regression 12 — Language Style and Mathematical Writing Style Are Distinct

Input: a long mathematical style corpus.

Expected:
- build `LanguageStyleLedger` for sentence cadence, transitions, mathematical verbs, certainty, and display grammar;
- build `MathematicalWritingStyleLedger` for proof motion, obstruction handling, lemma ecology, justification locality, generality bridges, estimates, and closure;
- do not collapse these into vague labels such as "elegant" or "clear";
- unsupported traits are omitted or marked tentative.
