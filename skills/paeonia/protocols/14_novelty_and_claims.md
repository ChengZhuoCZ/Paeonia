# Protocol 14 — Novelty, Priority, and Strength of Claims

## Rule

Writing quality must not inflate mathematical or historical claims.

Distinguish:
- “we prove” — established in this manuscript;
- “we obtain as a consequence” — derived from proved material;
- “to our knowledge” — requires literature checking appropriate to the claim;
- “first/sharp/optimal/complete” — requires particularly careful verification.

If verification is incomplete, use a neutral factual description of the result rather than an unsupported priority adjective.
