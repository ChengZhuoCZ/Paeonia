# Literature, Credit, and Grand Introduction — Release Checklist

## Research before prose (RECONSTRUCT)

- [ ] Closed theorem fingerprint identifies object, scope, strongest conclusion, obstruction and aliases.
- [ ] Live scholarly retrieval used at least two independent routes beyond the supplied bibliography when permitted.
- [ ] Origin/benchmark, exact predecessors, strongest (possibly incomparable) frontier, method ancestry and recent/parallel work are identified or access gaps disclosed.
- [ ] Central comparisons inspect the relevant primary-source statements, not just abstracts or snippets.
- [ ] Metadata, version/locator and adjacent-claim support are separate statuses; corrected/retracted items are identified when material.
- [ ] Current research date, search routes, verified sources, exclusions and unresolved claims are recorded in one compact dossier.

## Adequate and fair credit

- [ ] Earlier authors are named for what they actually achieved, including exact prior results and imported methods.
- [ ] The introduction does not imply that the present authors originated previously established ideas.
- [ ] Parallel, independent, contemporary and genuinely competing lines are treated fairly and not erased by a single historical arc.
- [ ] Priority words have specific, versioned evidence; failed searching alone is not used to claim `first`.
- [ ] Citations are concentrated around their real mathematical roles, not padded or restricted by an arbitrary count.

## Grand narrative with a truth ceiling

- [ ] A real parent mathematical program was tested, with either a supported connection or a stated reason for choosing `DIRECT`.
- [ ] The opening establishes a concrete mathematical stake, not a generic declaration of importance.
- [ ] Classical principle → exact frontier → nonroutine obstruction → theorem is a defensible causal spine where relevant.
- [ ] The conceptual mechanism and the specific program-level meaning are both legible.
- [ ] Every historical or adjacent passage contributes to the reader's understanding; no delaying of the theorem for drama.
- [ ] Strong descriptors match the result's actual class, scale, parameters, and proof boundaries.
