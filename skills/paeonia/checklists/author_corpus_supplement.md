# Author-corpus supplement checklist

- [ ] The proof-bearing source is available and writing mode/scope are resolved.
- [ ] The author request was made once, or the author/opt-out was already supplied.
- [ ] Identity is resolved; ambiguous names were not guessed.
- [ ] Retrieval used only legal open-access or author-provided copies.
- [ ] Exact versions, locators and SHA-256 hashes are recorded.
- [ ] The default corpus contains 3–6 papers and explains topic, centrality, recency and authorship balance.
- [ ] Author-prepared versions are preferred for prose analysis.
- [ ] Complete Introductions and functionally varied body windows were sampled.
- [ ] Coauthored evidence is labeled author-associated rather than individually attributed.
- [ ] Stable traits recur across papers and, where possible, coauthor configurations.
- [ ] Tentative, collaboration-specific and topic-specific traits are not promoted to defaults.
- [ ] The corpus affects realization only; theorem facts and novelty claims remain source-controlled.
- [ ] Style papers are not inserted as citations without Protocol 07 verification.
- [ ] Existing local-polish prose remains the primary baseline.
- [ ] No phrase bank, sentence template, distinctive tic or paragraph sequence is stored.
- [ ] Corpus is quarantined before drafting and final prose passes overlap review when applicable.
