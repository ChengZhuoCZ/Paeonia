# Editorial Positioning Checklist for Research Introductions

## Ambition

- [ ] `NarrativeAmbitionLedger` identifies the strongest defensible mathematical position.
- [ ] Scale is selected from STANDARD / EXPANSIVE / GRAND / GRAND_PLUS by evidence, not requested prestige.
- [ ] Every strong claim has a theorem or literature anchor.
- [ ] Verified central strong claims are marked `must_surface`.
- [ ] The Introduction does not weaken a verified conjecture resolution, global theorem, complete classification, sharp result, or closed parameter range into vague prose.

## Historical/frontier positioning

- [ ] History ends at the exact frontier immediately before the present work.
- [ ] The unresolved gap is explicit.
- [ ] Historical depth is proportional to explanatory value rather than age of the subject.
- [ ] Adjacent theories are included only when they sharpen the present mathematical position.

## Amplification

- [ ] Context is broadened only to a mathematically operative scale.
- [ ] The contrast between known and new is explicit.
- [ ] Structural obstruction is explained when it is part of why the theorem matters.
- [ ] Theorem interpretation states what changes in the mathematical picture.
- [ ] Methodological afterlife is stated at exactly the verified level.
- [ ] Broader consequences are surfaced when supported.

## Factuality ceiling

- [ ] Every potentially strong wording has a verified scope.
- [ ] No novelty, priority, completeness, sharpness, or transferability claim exceeds evidence.
- [ ] Prestige adjectives are not substituting for mathematical relations.
- [ ] The main result is not delayed for rhetorical drama.

## Editorial impact

- [ ] A specialist editor can identify big problem, exact gap, exact advance, and theorem strength from the first part of the Introduction.
- [ ] `EDITORIAL-IMPACT` passes.
- [ ] `GRAND-NARRATIVE-INTEGRITY` still passes.
- [ ] `INTRODUCTION-PROSE-INTEGRITY` still passes.
