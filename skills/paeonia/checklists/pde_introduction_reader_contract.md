# PDE Introduction Reader Contract — Conditional Checklist

Apply only the items that are material to the theorem.

## Theorem coordinates

- [ ] The governing equation or formulation is identifiable.
- [ ] Dimension, domain/geometry, and relevant boundary or initial conditions are clear.
- [ ] The scaling, threshold, endpoint, or operative regime is visible when it controls the result.
- [ ] The solution class and convergence/regularity topology are explicit where they affect the claim.
- [ ] The conclusion type and theorem architecture—single result, dichotomy, stratification, hierarchy, bridge, or solution-class separation—are recoverable.

## Positioning

- [ ] The result delta is stated on concrete axes rather than by “improves” or “extends” alone.
- [ ] Incomparable prior results are not forced into a false linear ranking.
- [ ] Known counterexamples, open regimes, theorem boundaries, and method boundaries are distinguished.
- [ ] Optimality is explained by an example, threshold, scaling argument, or sharpness statement when supported.

## Assumptions and mechanism

- [ ] Nonstandard assumptions are classified as structural, technical, normalization, or proof-local when that distinction matters.
- [ ] A major assumption's role is tied to the proof stage where useful.
- [ ] The obstruction stack is causal and contains no more layers than the reader needs.
- [ ] Any decisive formula is interpreted in the original variables and connected to the control it supplies.
- [ ] The proof overview names the new interface and compresses standard PDE machinery.

## Genre-specific closure

- [ ] Global existence: the a priori estimate is linked to the continuation criterion.
- [ ] Stability/scattering: the linear mechanism is linked to nonlinear closure and the final asymptotic topology.
- [ ] Threshold result: the threshold object and both sides of the dichotomy are correctly located.
- [ ] Regularity/stratification: the branches, singular-set conclusion, and optimality boundary are visible.
- [ ] Singular limit: topology, multiplicity, concentration, or defect is addressed when relevant.
- [ ] Nonuniqueness: the exact solution and admissibility class is not overstated.
- [ ] Dual audience: the object dictionary is minimal and theorem-facing rather than two parallel surveys.

## Compression

- [ ] The explanatory burden, not journal prestige, determined length and theorem placement.
- [ ] No PDE coordinate is included merely to make the Introduction look comprehensive.
