# Checklist — Paragraph Authorial Rhythm

- [ ] Each important paragraph break corresponds to a mathematical state change.
- [ ] Consecutive routine paragraphs are not fragmented without reason.
- [ ] Delicate legality/uniformity/endpoint paragraphs are locally explicit.
- [ ] Bridge paragraphs state the obstruction that forces the change of method.
- [ ] Routine paragraphs are not expanded merely to equalize paragraph sizes.
- [ ] Paragraph openings are not dominated by repeated administrative signposting.
- [ ] Displays are integrated by mathematical purpose and consequence.
- [ ] Closure contracts rather than triggering a generic recap.
- [ ] Role sequences differ when proof structure differs.
- [ ] No source paragraph sequence or numerical fingerprint is used as a generation template.
