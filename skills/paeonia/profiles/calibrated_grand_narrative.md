# Calibrated Grand Narrative Profile

## Register

Ambitious in mathematical positioning, exact in factual scope, and engaged in mathematical judgment. A supported phrase such as “a surprising connection” can identify what deserves attention; nearby mathematics must explain the judgment. Neither neutral tone nor adjective counts are a target.

## Editorial posture

Do not ask `How can this sound important?` Ask:

`What is the largest mathematical story this theorem and the verified literature genuinely support?`

Then tell that story clearly.

## Strong-result policy

Verified strong statements are not stylistic liabilities. If the paper resolves, settles, completes, closes, sharpens, or globalizes a known problem in a precisely verified sense, say so plainly.

Underclaiming is a defect when it hides the central contribution.


## Two independent axes

Calibrate mathematical ambition and scientific-context depth separately. A `GRAND_PLUS` theorem can be purely mathematical and need no physical background; a model-driven PDE paper can need a `PHYSICS_LED` opening even when its mathematical ambition is only `EXPANSIVE`. Specialist readership compresses routine definitions, not scientifically operative context.

When a genuine model lineage exists, grandeur may come from the full chain

`phenomenon/model → mathematical structure → distinguished regime → classical frontier → present theorem → calibrated interpretation`.

This chain is evidence-driven. Generic phrases such as `arises in physics` do not count as context.

## Grandness

Grandness is built from:

- canonical problem scale;
- historical/conjectural depth;
- exact prior frontier;
- structural obstruction;
- theorem strength;
- change in mathematical picture;
- proof mechanism and its afterlife.

It is not built from `deep`, `fundamental`, `major`, or `breakthrough` used without mathematical content.

## Paragraph pressure

A strong Introduction may spend real space on history or adjacent theory when those sections create pressure toward the theorem. It should not distribute space evenly. The exact frontier, obstruction, main theorem, and consequence may deserve disproportionate weight.

## Editorial first-two-pages test

A specialist editor should be able to identify the big problem, exact gap, exact advance, and strongest defensible consequence before reaching the technical body.

## Factuality ceiling

Every amplification has a ceiling. Stronger prose is encouraged up to that ceiling and forbidden beyond it.
