The expressive force comes from the distance between the starting objects, the visible reason they meet, and the capability gained. An adjective can direct attention to this relation, but the relation must carry the emphasis. Neither a required phrase nor adjective removal reproduces the effect.

## How to compose from this lesson

After reading the proof, identify the mathematical relation the reader should remember. Draft that explanation with only the objects needed to see it. A useful progression is original meaning, operative identity or construction, and the new control it reveals. Stop this conceptual account at the first serious remaining obstacle. Then explain the route that overcomes it. This is a choice of explanatory resolution, not a required pair of subsections or a ban on starting with a conditional assembly.

Let equations do work in the story: define the encoding, show why a norm or exponent is selected, reveal a sign or equality case, or explain why an inadequate bound cannot close the argument. A sentence after a calculation should interpret its new consequence. Avoid repeating every symbol or surrounding one calculation with a catalogue of technical labels.

Plan the full proof dependencies privately. In the prose, combine steps with the same conceptual job. Distinguish repeated uses of the same tool when their inputs and purposes differ: in this example, recovering a common cubic leading value obtains the mesh, whereas a later real-part comparison retains the defect on that mesh. Omitting this distinction creates a false sense that regularity was already available. Listing every frequency sector would create a different problem by hiding the distinction in detail.

## What not to learn from the sample

The preferred original pages were not a correctness certificate. Their complex-valued cubic claim, implicit phase assumptions, and compressed first bootstrap needed correction. Keep assumptions where the reader first needs their consequence; a later prose qualification cannot justify silently strengthening a displayed result. An exact phase-model identity does not automatically become an exact identity for weighted finite columns.

The earlier fourth page's opening convention paragraph was a response to the user's instruction to freeze the preceding prose, but the extensive rewrite lost interpretive paragraphs the user wanted retained. It was subsequently replaced by a restoration with local repairs. That history is evidence against equating more technical coverage with a better Introduction. In free composition, place normalization and centering near their original use; in a preserved baseline, make only the authorized local correction. The user's page boundary is a local editing constraint, not a four-page target for other articles.

Do not force every paper to have a Fourier bridge, a cubic cancellation, two scales, a new introductory proposition, or a conjecture narrative. For broader audiences, an existing interpretation can give real access without a new theorem; a usable quantitative result can be foregrounded when it helps. Select the form from the mathematics and the user's preferred level of exposition.

Apply these choices through Protocols 04p and 04e, then use 04q to check both what the reader can explain and which complications the conceptual account has honestly left for the outline. This profile supports an editorial judgment; it does not predict future reader comprehension.
