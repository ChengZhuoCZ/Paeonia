## Supported whole-work traits

### 1. Global orientation and technical execution are separate

The source repeatedly gives a high-level conceptual map before the technical development that realizes it. In research-paper projection, this supports early exposure of the main result and proof mechanism while allowing the actual proof to activate later, after the necessary engines are available.

### 2. Mathematical mass is pressure-driven

Major units vary strongly in length and formal-result density. Some units carry broad conceptual or geometric development with relatively low theorem density; others are dense culminations or technical engines. The research-paper analogue is to let central proof sections dominate when the proof graph requires it rather than equalizing section mass.

### 3. Global architecture may contain technical peaks

The source exhibits chapters that function as concentrated technical or theorem-dense peaks instead of distributing difficult mathematics evenly. In a paper, choose `COMPACT_PEAK`, `TWO_PEAK`, `CASCADE`, `REGIME_CLUSTER`, or another technical-core shape only from the target proof dependencies.

### 4. Supporting mathematics may deserve real appendices

The source appendices contain serious analysis and topology, showing that appendix status does not imply mathematical triviality. The transferable rule is modularity: supporting theory may be isolated when it is self-contained, methodologically orthogonal, reusable, or too interruptive for the main spine.

### 5. The end of the mathematics controls the end of the work

The source does not suggest a universal need for symmetric concluding units after every major development. In research-paper projection, a paper may close directly after the main proof, rigidity/classification endgame, or final consequence.

## Research-paper realization

Preferred global policy:

- expose the main theorem early once minimal notation is available;
- delay proof activation only for genuine prerequisites;
- reconstruct the body around one target-induced proof spine or an explicitly justified multi-engine topology;
- allow technical mass to concentrate;
- compress the Introduction semantically rather than by a source-derived ratio;
- keep mechanism-bearing novelty and equality/rigidity in the reader-facing body;
- use appendices only after a modularity test;
- suppress ceremonial symmetry and generic conclusion prose.
