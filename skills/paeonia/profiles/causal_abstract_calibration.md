## What the reference makes possible

The result gives the admissible class and exact uniqueness. Quantitative stability then has a precise limitation: closeness to the disk leaves the polygon's discrete geometry unresolved. The exterior-angle measure retains that geometry; the inverse-cubic inequality identifies the regular configuration. One defect connects this discrete equality case with the quadratic and nonlinear spectral comparison. Uniform control at arbitrarily small defects permits an exact geometric conclusion.

The explanatory strength comes from the successive uses of information, not from the number of sentences, the particular Fourier exponent or a vocabulary of emphatic adjectives. Another paper may have no useful defect functional, no two-scale argument and no equality case to advertise.
